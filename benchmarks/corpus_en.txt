Nortlann Island Nature and Resource Guide

Zerbalit is a metal. A metal is a substance. Zerbalit is a substance.
Kelvit is a metal. Kelvit is a substance.
Torvanit is a metal. Zerbalit is blue. Kelvit is grey.
If torvanit is heated, a green flame comes out.

Vorlin is a drink. A drink is a liquid. Selvin is a drink.
Selvin is a liquid. Vorlin is made from the norgul plant.
Selvin is drunk cold. Vorlin is drunk hot.

Morlan is a bird. A bird is a creature. Morlan is a creature.
Zilfen is a bird. Karvel is a fish. A fish is a creature.
Karvel is a creature. Morlan flies at night. Zilfen flies by day.
Karvel lives in deep water. Morlan eats norgul seeds.

Norgul is a plant. A plant is a creature. Norgul is a creature.
Telvas is a plant. Telvas is a creature. Norgul grows in the marsh.
Telvas grows on the rocks. Norgul opens white flowers.
Telvas opens red flowers.

Nortlann is an island. Velmar is a village. Doruk is a village.
Velmar is in the north of the island. Doruk is in the south of the island.
Zerbalit is mined near Velmar. Kelvit is mined near Doruk.

If it rains, the marsh grows. If the marsh grows, norgul multiplies.
If norgul multiplies, morlan multiplies. If there is a drought, telvas dries.

The island has four seasons. In winter snow falls. In summer storms happen.
In the village of Velmar people fish. In the village of Doruk people mine.
In the middle of the island there is a large lake. The lake's water is fresh.
Karvel is seen in the lake too. Zilfen nests on the lake shore.
The island is reached by boat. The island looks small on maps.
The guide is updated every year. The islanders write the guide.
